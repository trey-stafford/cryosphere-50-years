# 50 Years of Cryospheric Data

Plot/visualize 50 years of Cryosphere data (1976 - 2026) in honor of the
National Snow and Ice Data Center's (NSIDC) 50th anniversary.

This repository sets out to find and visualize datasets covering the period
1976-2026 that are archived at NSIDC.

More about NSIDC's 50th anniversary:
<https://nsidc.org/about/about-nsidc/what-we-do/50th-anniversary>


## Candidate Datasets

* [Nenana Ice Classic: Tanana River Ice Annual Breakup Dates, Version 2](https://nsidc.org/data/nsidc-0064/versions/2)
* [Canadian Ice Service Arctic Regional Sea Ice Charts in SIGRID-3 Format, Version 2](https://nsidc.org/data/g02171/versions/2)
